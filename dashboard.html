        Locations and time zone

        The day closes at midnight and deposits arrive at 6:00 am in each location's own time. Payroad serves TX, NM, AZ, NV and CA: Central (most of Texas), Mountain (El Paso area, New Mexico), Arizona (no daylight saving) and Pacific (Nevada, California).

        Users
